**SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production**



**Overview**



This project was completed as a formative group task focused on Sustainable Development Goal 12 (SDG 12): Responsible Consumption and Production.



The project involved researching the topic and presenting relevant information using supporting sources, including material from the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO).



**Project Aim**



The aim was to explore responsible consumption and production and communicate relevant findings through a structured group presentation.



**Project Format**



1. Collaborative PowerPoint presentation

2\. Research using external sources

3\. Academic citations and referencing

4\. Organisation and communication of information



**Skills Demonstrated**



1. Teamwork and collaboration

2\. Research and information gathering

3\. Academic writing and referencing

4\. Presentation and communication skills

5\. Working to a short deadline



**Project Files**



SDG-12-Responsible-Consumption-Presentation.pptx — the original group presentation, including its contributors and references.



**Reflection**



This project provided an opportunity to apply academic research and referencing skills in a collaborative setting. It also developed experience organising information and communicating findings within a short timeframe.
