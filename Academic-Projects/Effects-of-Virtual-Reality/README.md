**Effects of Virtual Reality**



**Overview**



This was an individual academic project completed as part of the Extended Projects module. It explored the effects of virtual reality and involved researching information, documenting the research process, and presenting findings.



**Project Objectives**



1. Investigate the effects of virtual reality.

2\. Gather and organise information from relevant sources.

3\. Document the research process and findings.

4\. Present information in a clear and structured format.



**Project Files**



1. Effects-of-Virtual-Reality.pptx — presentation covering the effects of virtual reality and how information was gathered.

2\. VR-Research-Planning.pdf — document outlining the planning process behind the research.



**Skills Demonstrated**



1. Independent research and information gathering

2\. Source evaluation and referencing

3\. Academic writing and presentation

4\. Organisation and planning

5\. Communicating findings clearly



**Reflection**



This project provided experience in managing an individual research task, documenting the research process, and communicating findings through a structured presentation and supporting planning document.
